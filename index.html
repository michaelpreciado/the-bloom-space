Welcome

A room that feels like an exhale

I built The Bloom Space because Visalia deserves a place where celebrations feel personal, not packaged. Every corner was chosen to make you feel held—from the soft blush walls to the eucalyptus that catches the afternoon light.

Whether you’re planning a baby shower with twenty of your closest friends or a bridal shower that’s been months in the making, this room meets you where you are. No venue minimums that don’t make sense. No blank white boxes. Just a room that already looks like your Pinterest board.

Come see it in person. Viewings are by appointment only—that way the space is quiet, the light is right, and we can talk through exactly what you need.

— Araceli, owner & lead decor artist

A note about deposits

We require both a retainer deposit and a security deposit to lock in your booking. Both are non-refundable. The retainer reserves your date and time; the security deposit covers any incidental damages. We keep our deposits policy simple and upfront so there are never surprises. If you have questions, just ask—we’re happy to walk through it.

Weekly Bachata Class

Every Monday at 7:30pm, the room is reserved for our weekly Bachata class. If you’d like to book a Monday evening, please plan around this time slot.

FAQ

Everything you need to know

We require both a retainer deposit and a security deposit to hold your date. Both are non-refundable. The retainer reserves your time slot; the security deposit covers any incidental damages. We keep this simple and transparent—no hidden fees, no surprises. The security deposit is forfeited if damages exceed that amount.

Since both deposits are non-refundable, cancellations mean forfeiting those amounts. We recommend you only book once you’re confident about your date. If you need to reschedule, reach out—we’ll do our best to accommodate based on availability, though rescheduling is not guaranteed.

Yes—your rental time includes both setup and clean-up. Please plan to have everything set up and taken down within your booked window. If you need extra time, let us know in advance and we can extend your booking at the hourly rate if the schedule allows.

Yes. We have on-site parking available for guests. The venue is located at 316 S Bridge St, Visalia, and is easy to find. Additional street parking is available nearby for larger events.

Yes, you may serve alcohol at your event, provided no alcohol is sold on the premises. You are responsible for ensuring all guests are of legal drinking age. Please drink responsibly and arrange for designated drivers or ride shares as needed.

Absolutely. You are welcome to bring your own food, drinks, and decorations. We only ask that you avoid anything that could damage the walls, floors, or furniture—no confetti, glitter, or open flames without prior approval. The owner is a balloon and event decor artist herself, so she knows the drill!

Basic clean-up is included in your rental time. We ask that you dispose of large trash items, wipe down surfaces, and leave the space as you found it. Excessive mess or damage may be charged against the security deposit.

The Bloom Space comfortably accommodates up to 65 guests. This includes both seated and standing configurations. For events with tables and chairs, we recommend adjusting the count slightly to allow for comfortable movement.

Your rental includes access to the full event space, tables and chairs for up to 65 guests, use of the kitchenette, WiFi, Bluetooth speaker system, restroom facilities, and climate control. Decor, tableware, and catering are not included—bring your own vision to life!
